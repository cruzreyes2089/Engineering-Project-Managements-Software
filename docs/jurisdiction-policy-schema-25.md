# Jurisdiction policy — schema 25

Canonical permitting jurisdiction labels are locally maintained by an administrator with Compliance.Policy. No names or firm facts are seeded. EligibleForScreening, Restricted and Unverified events are append-only; missing entries are held. Each proposal/project review binds the exact current jurisdiction event, and a new event invalidates earlier clearance for that label even if later re-enabled. Screening remains separately required for the exact proposal snapshot and project. ASCII English labels compare case-insensitively; the operator must independently establish municipal boundaries, aliases, permit authority and overlapping employer restrictions. This is not a geocoding or legal clearance system. Old reviews without policy evidence remain historical and held. No executable distribution or production transactions.


Project registers, task planning, staff schedule and own-work views expose only the current conflict status, not confidential evidence. Authorized compliance history separately displays recorded result, reviewer ID and exact jurisdiction event ID. Current status may differ from historical recorded result after a policy change.

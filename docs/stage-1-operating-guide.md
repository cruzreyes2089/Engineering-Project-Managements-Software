# Stage 1 operating guide — qualification draft

**Security HOLD. Synthetic data only.** These procedures describe intended operation and are not evidence of successful desktop execution. Use only an independently cleared exact build in an isolated approved Windows test environment. No current production installer or production startup mode exists.

## Initial setup and administration
For controlled acceptance follow desktop-acceptance-workspace.md. Record the exact commit, complete inventory digest, environment and session ID; bootstrap a fictional firm and its own client prefix. Keep test credentials out of source, logs and evidence. Use distinct administrator, employee and reviewer accounts. In Staff & permissions, register employees separately from user accounts, assign only necessary grants, set temporary credentials, and require password change. Review access and license expiry periodically. Avoid sharing accounts. Sign out before changing operators.

## Client to project workflow
In Clients & contacts, create or select the exact client and verify client number before adding contacts. Keep inactive clients historical. In Proposals & projects, prepare scope and pricing, review conflict and party evidence, freeze and record issuance, and register acceptance. Acceptance creates a pending project, not authorization to work. Record reviewed signed agreement evidence and explicit authorization before starting work. Use synthetic agreements until approved legal templates are available. Complete the project setup checklist; maintain phases, tasks, assignments and dates. Record document copies and QA/QC evidence; folder templates are logical configuration only, not physical directory creation. Record additional scope separately and obtain authorization before work.

## Employee and reviewer workflow
Employees use My work and My time & expenses. Record attendance and actual time separately, with accurate dates and task references; submit expenses with appropriate evidence. Use corrections rather than overwriting reviewed facts. Independent reviewers review submissions; billing classification is a distinct authorized action. Rejected, unapproved and unclassified entries must not silently become billable. Staff without financial grants must not see rates or restricted reports. Review pending work and QA items before closeout; historical corrections remain subject to eligibility and closure cutoffs.

## Internal billing and reports
Establish the accepted billing policy and caps, select eligible approved classified work, prepare an internal invoice draft, and review and approve under the documented permission and independence requirements. Held drafts are not exportable. Do not treat an internal draft as an issued invoice, receipt or balance. Manager remains the authoritative accounting system; automated posting and actual settlement are outside Stage 1. Compare authorized operational and internal financial reports against reviewed source entries, dates and allocations. Do not infer cash receipts or authoritative AR from draft totals.

## Recovery and data preservation
Authorized administrator: create a recovery copy in protected local storage outside the install directory. Use a new filename and retain the displayed SHA-256 in a separately protected record. The copy contains confidential records including password hashes; accounting credentials are removed and posting is held. Verify recovery using Restore to separate file with the trusted hash and a new destination. Never replace the active database or assume a successful isolated restore is a completed live recovery. Upgrade rehearsals use protected copies; do not perform a live schema upgrade without an approved procedure and verified backup. No automated purge of user data or recovery copies. Record recovery results privately.

## Operational incident handling
If sign-in or startup fails, stop, preserve the workspace and record the exact nonsecret error. Do not select another database, delete sidecars, modify the schema, reinstall over an unknown directory or bypass security warnings. If an operation reports failure, refresh the relevant register before retrying; do not assume the write either succeeded or failed without checking. For integrity or permission discrepancies preserve evidence and restrict access; do not distribute the executable or disclose client data. A suspected compromised build requires independent review.

# Staff workload scenario — PRJ-02

Read-only firm-wide staff workload from current scheduled open tasks. Same transactional planning/project/employee permission requirements as staff schedule, bounded 366 inclusive days, 500 employees and 500 overlapping tasks; no separate financial grants, rates, actual work or payroll. Include active employees without assignments, and inactive employees with outstanding overlapping tasks (held). Completed, cancelled and discontinued tasks excluded. Current active project, clear conflict state, base/authorized scope and active employee are planning eligibility indicators only, not independent permission to start. Other open tasks held. No historical as-of reconstruction or overdue tasks outside the chosen overlap.

Spread each original estimate across all weekdays of its original task interval as integer minutes, earliest weekdays receive remainder; sum allocations only within the review window. This is not remaining effort, daily schedule commitment, actual utilization or billing. Weekend-only tasks have their full estimate listed as unallocated; these figures are not additive across overlapping or disjoint partial windows that touch the same weekend-only task. No holiday, leave, overhead, part-time or individual capacity policy inferred. Optional common minutes per weekday is entered explicitly (blank by default); compare eligible daily allocated minutes against that assumption only, count strictly overloaded weekdays and show no capacity for inactive staff. No aggregate workload % or claim of staffing sufficiency. No schema changes, automatic rescheduling, actual accounting or production release. Tests cover pure allocation and current lifecycle/grant integration. Interactive desktop and exact-revision Windows CI required; original PRJ-02 acceptance still not executed.

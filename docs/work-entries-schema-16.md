# Attendance, work facts and billing review — schema 16

One open attendance session per active linked employee; UTC clock facts, immutable close-once history and rollback-clock guard. Attendance is not hours billed. Employee time/expense submissions require own started task, authorized project, date inside planned task and not in future. Positive integer minutes or exact USD cents; immutable facts, stable operation key and complete payload comparison, per-day 24-hour nonrejected time limit. Rejected submissions remain historical; replacement is a new fact. Independent reviewer approves/rejects; finance classification is separate and only for approved work. No employee-facing billing/rate field, no self-review or self-classification even with grants. No reimbursement, invoice, receipt attachment, overtime/payroll computation, reversal or actual accounting writes. A sole-owner exception is deliberately not implemented; production policy needs explicit decision later. Local OS time is not authenticated. Project date limits and provisional expense categories are development choices. No executable distribution; CI required.

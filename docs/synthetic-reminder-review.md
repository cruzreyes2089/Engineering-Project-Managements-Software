# Synthetic reminder preparation

The dedicated due-date fixture review now permits selection of a strictly overdue invoice, followed by a new authenticated GET-only acquisition using the one-time local token. The prior and fresh observation must have the same marker, exact invoice identity, customer, issue date, amount, outstanding balance and due date; the new observation must be later and within two minutes of preparation. A changed balance/date/identity requires refresh; missing, due-today and not-yet-due evidence is not eligible. Display an internal test-only subject/body with invoice reference, dates, balance, days overdue and observation timestamp; no recipient, payment instructions, external process, email delivery, persistence or posting. UI/service uses existing financial and identity permissions and rechecks after acquisition. Scope is the exact dedicated synthetic business; production reminder templates, recipient verification, sent history, email provider integration and actual desktop acceptance remain pending. The system deliberately does not infer real-world payment status from a draft or cached data.


Service-level regression exercises a separate one-time credential alongside an already enrolled original profile, counts the two independent ten-GET acquisitions, verifies unchanged profile and marker identity, and checks denied confirmation and revoked financial permission. No actual Manager or email operation occurs in CI.

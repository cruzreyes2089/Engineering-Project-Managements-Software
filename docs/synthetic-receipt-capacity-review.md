# Receipt allocation capacity review — offline synthetic candidate

The receipt capacity review takes two independently supplied normalized synthetic ledger snapshots, checks the enrolled marker, completeness, declared USD scope, reconciles every invoice, receipt, payment and summary, and compares canonical ordered snapshot fingerprints. It validates the exact receipt intent against its customer, invoice, receivable account and receiving cash account. Zero/negative, fractional-cent, excess, wrong-customer/account and duplicate evidence are rejected. Successful review returns projected remaining invoice capacity and a digest bound to the entire stable snapshot and exact wire intent. A new receipt or changed intent invalidates the earlier review even if enough capacity remains.

The review is not a reservation of funds, a remote concurrency lock, a receipt posting adapter, or proof of independently obtained evidence. Another provider writer could still consume capacity after review. It cannot authorize send, retry or accounting state transitions. No integration into the live transport, invoice balances or financial reports; no schema changes. Existing fixture scope remains narrow (zero opening balances, one USD currency, no taxes, credits, discounts or retainer applications).

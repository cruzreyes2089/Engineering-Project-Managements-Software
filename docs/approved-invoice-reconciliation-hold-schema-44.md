# Approved invoice reconciliation hold — proposed schema 44

Dependent on schema 43 candidate 14c15a9. An approved internal draft may have been exported, manually issued in Manager or paid. CRC cannot assume external status, and a failed match is not proof of nonissuance. Introduce a protective exception record, not a reversal.

- Exact project, approved draft and immutable payload hash, authorized invoice editor + approver with project/client visibility, explicit confirmation and discrepancy evidence. Any authorized editor/approver (including founding owner who prepared the draft) can impose a protective hold; no authority to issue, reverse or release it. One immutable hold per draft. Record actor and UTC timestamp and atomic audit.
- Keep original state Approved, lines, hash, source allocations and contractual caps. Separate hold flag in register and detail; report held amount/count as subset of approved internal drafts, never subtract from actual receivables or treat as posted credit. Block new CSV and DOCX preparation and retrieval of previously generated review document bytes through normal export workflows; preserve metadata and underlying bytes for later authorized forensic review. Previously exported files cannot be recalled by software. Read-only Manager comparison remains permitted for investigation; no external writes or automatic issue state.
- Explicit management UI to record/view reason and warning. No release or cancellation of approved held drafts until independent external settlement/issuance evidence and reconciliation are designed. Other unaffected drafts may continue; no release of sources or cap.
- Test-first stubs intentionally fail. Add rollback, concurrent hold, direct SQL and migration/restore tests. Full Windows green before integration, desktop acceptance and security qualification still pending. No main merge, production data, provider mutation or distribution.

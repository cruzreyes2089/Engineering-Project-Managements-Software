# Operational invoice preparation — schema 19

Manager remains the authority for posted accounting. The CRC draft register is not a general ledger, AR register, or payment authority. Explicit immutable contract billing policy (fixed, hourly, hybrid; optional total cap and expense permission), confidential effective-dated employee billing/cost rates, approved billable work candidates, exact cents, frozen internal draft numbers and payload hashes, atomic source allocation and cap checking. Unapproved work and extra scope with unconfigured commercial terms cannot be billed. Unsent draft cancellation preserves history and releases sources; approved drafts cannot be silently cancelled. No tax, discounts, retainers, external posting, client delivery, financial-report certification or amendments yet. No default rates are inferred. Rate/term changes require explicit evidence; cost rates are never in invoice or ordinary employee projections. No new executable uploads. Synthetic tests added; CI pending.

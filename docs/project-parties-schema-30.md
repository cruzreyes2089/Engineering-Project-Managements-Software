# Project parties — schema 30

A project participant register associates active CRM organizations/individuals and optional same-client contacts with an accepted project and exact accepted proposal hash. Role must be one of Client, Owner, Architect, Designer, Contractor, Consultant or Other; Client must match accepted engagement. Immutable recorded names and evidence; retirement retained separately; duplicate active party/role/contact combinations blocked in service and SQL. No auto merge or inferred participant identity. Changes invalidate completed ProjectContacts checklist atomically, not project authorization or conflict decisions. This is a participant inventory, not a legal declaration, verified authority to act, recipient permission or clearance. Party names may differ from current CRM; explicitly retire and re-register as needed. Future signed declaration evidence and linkage to conflict review are separate requirements. Existing grants protect reading and writing. Tests verify identity, historical labels, retirement, duplicates, stale checklist and permissions.

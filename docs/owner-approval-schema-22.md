# Single-owner approval exception — schema 22

Owner policy authorized by the project owner; no automatic activation on installation or migration. The founding account is identified by the unique native Bootstrap audit record and current active Administrator role. Legacy stores without that evidence cannot enable the exception. A separate opt-in event and justification are required. Each self-approval of time, expense, billing classification or internal invoice draft requires the existing decision permission, explicit individual confirmation, nonblank justification and a reference to the exact enabling event. Self-rejection is not included. Ordinary staff cannot use it. Another active account with any of the three approval grants (even pending a password change or missing ancillary grants) conservatively suspends the entire exception; suspension is persisted in the same transaction and audited. Removing the reviewer does not silently reactivate it. Owner eligibility changes also suspend it. Recovery copies disable the exception. Existing pre-schema-22 invoice approvals remain historical, not retroactively certified. No authorization to post or pay through Manager, no accounting security hold release, no real data. Local SQLite is not tamper-proof; OS access remains a separate boundary.

Read-only administrator audit shows policy events and self-decision justifications, actor, UTC, and enabling event IDs; historical unmarked invoice self-approvals are distinguished, not silently relabeled. Audit evidence also requires existing review/classification/invoice-view grants. The 500-row limits fail closed; pagination remains a development item. Tests exercise independent invoice review after suspension and deny ordinary staff self-approval.

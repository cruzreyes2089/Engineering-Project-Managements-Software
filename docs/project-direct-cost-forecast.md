# Restricted project direct-cost scenario

Isolated budget candidate only. Requires project view, Reports.Operations, Projects.Budget.View, Reports.Financial and Billing.Rates in one read transaction. Uses latest saved effort forecast only when its applicable budget and current raw effort snapshot match; stale or missing forecast rejected. All recorded valid approved/pending project time across dates is costed per submission using the employee's effective cost rate on the work date and existing cent-rounding convention. Rejected/invalidated records, overhead time, invoice drafts and other projects are excluded. Approved and pending project expenses are separate. Pending acceptance is assumed. Remaining unsubmitted labor uses explicit user-entered blended USD cost rate; future unsubmitted direct costs and total expected project revenue excluding tax are explicit, no assumed zero or revenue inferred. Missing recorded rates suppress total estimated direct cost and contribution, zero revenue suppresses percent. Result is estimated direct contribution (revenue less modeled direct costs), NOT net profit, accounting revenue, payroll, cash or authoritative Manager.io balance. Rates and expenses are current recorded facts, not frozen financial evidence; scenario is transient and may change on recalculation. No individual rate projection, persisted financial forecast, exports, actual accounting or schema expansion. Test covers calculations and actual service permissions, dated rates, pending/approved expenses, missing rates and stale evidence. Native and desktop qualification pending.

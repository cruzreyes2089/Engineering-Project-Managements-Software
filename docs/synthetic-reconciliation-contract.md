# Synthetic reconciliation domain contract (source-only)

The independent Manager Summary supplied by the owner matches cash 30, receivables 60, income 100, expense 10, profit 90, assets 90, liabilities 0 and equity 90. Only these fictional benchmark amounts are included in tests; no actual identifiers or screenshot are published.

The provider-neutral domain engine validates a deliberately narrow normalized scenario: zero opening balances, one common currency with two decimal places, service invoices credited to the selected income account, explicit customer/invoice receipt allocations into the selected cash account, and cash payments to the selected expense account. Distinct UUIDs, account roles, each individual invoice balance, aggregate ledger balances and independent statement equations are checked. Duplicate and mismatched relationships fail closed. Matching totals alone cannot pass mismatched invoice allocations. No dates, taxes, discounts, credit notes, opening balances, transfers, negative balances, FX, general ledger adjustments or production use. No automatic posting.

The fixture model is not wired into the Manager adapter or desktop UI. Actual detail field paths, exact account classification, relationship semantics, completeness and independent summary retrieval remain to be confirmed. Distribution hold remains unchanged; no binary uploads. An eventual adapter must verify scope, snapshot consistency, currency, data completeness, independent summary and applicable accounting policy before invoking any production reconciliation. The synthetic evaluator does not prove that any real API record has those relationships.

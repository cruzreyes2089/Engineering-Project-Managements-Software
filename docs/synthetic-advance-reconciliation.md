# Refundable unearned advances — synthetic accounting reference model

The new offline reconciler models refundable unearned deposits as liabilities on receipt. Application against an issued accrual invoice reduces both receivables and the advance liability without new cash or income; refund of unapplied amounts reduces cash and liability. It checks exact deposit, customer, project and invoice relationships, positive cent amounts, unique document/movement keys, deposit dates, individual reported balances and the independent summary. Direct invoice receipts and advance applications share invoice capacity. The reference model excludes fees earned on receipt, taxes, credits, expenses, other cash movements, opening balances and reversal of prior applications. It is a bounded synthetic end-state reconciliation, not a chronological bank ledger or an assertion of Manager API behavior. No production account mapping, legal retainer classification, posting, refund or application authority. Schema unchanged; exact revision CI pending.
